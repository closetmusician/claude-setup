## Why it works

- He starts by guessing the "ask behind the ask": no non-compliant models, and the customer's own record keeping.
- He sets two limits: model updates go in general release notes, and product makes no one-customer commitments.
- He puts any special handling on the account team: "AE/CSM needs to assume that responsibility."
- He routes parts to owners: the platform team for model notes, a colleague for contract wording.
- The follow-ups are short approvals: "I'm comfortable w/ it", then "you're good to go".
- He closes with thanks for the "partnership".
