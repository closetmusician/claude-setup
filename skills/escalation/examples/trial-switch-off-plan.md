## Situation

<ops lead> reports <n> accounts with GovernAI trials still on after expiry. <n> of them use it.
The GM says product must build a process to turn trials off.
Yu-Kuan sets the data ask, a short-term plan and a ship date.

## Sent

### A. <date>

`````text
<ops lead> if you/your team could provide the list of <n> accounts w/ their info like account id, account names, site names (short site names - this is the most important) etc we could do a 1 time bulk turn off for these folks. Would you mind providing it to @<eng lead> once you have the list.

In the future we’ll build a more automated shut off within the product, but the above approach will be faster in the short term.

Separately @<peer exec> - re: the campaign to go after these customers after we shut them off, I’ll leave that in your team’s hands to do so?

YL
`````

### B. <date>

`````text
Hey <peer exec>, my team is planning the programmatic AI trial shut off already and will report back shortly w/ the timeline.

In terms of when we actually shut them off, I don’t feel super dogmatic - I think your proposal of "auto shut off after <n> days from the day they activate considering the trial is <n> days” (so effectively we’ve given them a grace period of another <n> wks right?) sounds pretty good. If you’re good w/ that we can put that into practice pretty quickly within a few wks? Between that and our one-time shut off that we can commence at any time after the list has been scrubbed, I think we still have a good forcing function to push some bookings in the last <n> wks of <quarter> here. How does that sound?
`````

### C. <date> (to his own team)

`````text
Thanks all, end of week (Fri) would be good, since we need to give <GM> a response Mon morning on the potential timeline, e.g. when we’ll ship it. Please make sure <PM> & I know before you end the week?

I know this might compete w/ the onboarding work, so let us know what the competition tradeoffs are. I think we could probably do the manual approach until <date> when onboarding finishes. Then maybe spare 1 person to go finish this, maybe by EOY? As long as we ship it before <date> I think we’re good. Thanks team!
`````

## Why it works

- He states the data he needs and which field matters most ("short site names").
- He splits the plan: a one-time bulk shut-off now, and product automation later.
- He leaves the win-back campaign with <peer exec>, whose team runs the upgrade push. He phrases it as a question.
- He accepts the other leader's rule ("I don't feel super dogmatic") and ties it to <quarter> bookings.
- To his own team he sets a Friday deadline, asks for the tradeoffs, and sets the final date: before <date>.
