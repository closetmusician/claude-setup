## Why it works

- He opens with the legal fact and who confirmed it, with a screenshot.
- "By corollary" answers both questions in two numbered lines.
- When Security pushes again, he names the kind of concern: "not security blockers, but really good risk management points".
- He answers with actions that others own: <PMM> shares the messaging with <GC> for review, and the strategic accounts team handles big clients.
- He closes with a check question: "does that help <security lead>?"
- The last note routes a duplicate FAQ effort to the existing owner.
