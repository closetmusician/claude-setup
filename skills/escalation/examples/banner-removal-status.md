# banner-removal-status

```yaml
audience: boss or exec
reply_type: status to boss
channel: mail
words: 114
```

## Situation

A <$> account at churn risk demands removal of an AI banner.
The CEO asks if it is fixed.
The work is split across threads, so the exec group lacks the latest news.

## Sent

### <date>

`````text
Hey team, we have splintered threads so some of our working team updates from that other thread isn’t making it through here.


  1.
@<CS lead> if I could suggest - Let’s use this thread to report back up to ELT on our progress, and we’ll use the other one to handle the details & back & forth w/ the customer?
  2.
Re: banner - they reported back after their investigation this morning, and good news they may have found a workaround to hide it just for <customer>. The work is in progress and we’re tentatively aiming for early next week for release.

We will keep you all posted & updated on the progress,
YL
`````

## Why it works

- He names the process problem first: "we have splintered threads".
- He suggests one thread for exec updates and one for customer detail. He phrases it as a question to the CS lead.
- He gives the update as "good news" with a hedge: "they may have found a workaround".
- He gives a soft date: "tentatively aiming for early next week".
- He promises updates and stops. The note is about 110 words.
