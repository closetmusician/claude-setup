## Why it works

- He opens with a flat disagreement: "I disagree with <sales VP>'s characterization - this is not product's fault."
- A "TL:DR" line gives the verdict before the evidence.
- He builds a dated paper trail with links: the language page, the pinned post and the written "no" in <date>.
- He names the seller and the deal date to show the sale came after the warning.
- He recasts past fixes as goodwill: "this was us doing them a favor, never as a business case".
- He makes no admission and offers no next step. The ending is cut off in the sent text.
