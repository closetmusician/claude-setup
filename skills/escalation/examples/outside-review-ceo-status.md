## Why it works

- He opens with ownership: "I'm sorry you were caught off guard... our team owns the product experience."
- Two takeaways come first: the basics still need work, and customers like the AI.
- A table maps each complaint to why and to what we are doing. Some "why" cells point to customer settings or other teams.
- He uses a trend number (<n>% to <n>%) and says it will never reach 0. That resets expectations.
- He names a strategic choice (invest in Android or move to mobile web) and defers it with a date.
- He closes by asking to meet the investor's analysts. Criticism becomes a joint task.
