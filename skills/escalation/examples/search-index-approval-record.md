## Why it works

- He opens with the evidence itself: "This is the confirmation we received". The screenshots carry the argument.

- He states things flatly: "I don't know how it would have worked otherwise."
- He ends with a limited admission: communication could be stronger, and the team will run a retro. He does not say the decision was wrong.
- Point 2 stops mid-sentence ("We have also") in the sent text. It is kept as sent.
