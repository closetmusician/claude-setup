## Situation

The <region> commercial lead forwards an angry <customer> complaint to a Teams chat.
The customer reports "very low confidence in Diligent delivering". The customer wants web search off and Resource Centre indexed this week.
The real product date for the web search control is <date>.

## Sent

### <date>

`````text
Hi <sales lead>, thanks for the sharing the feedback from <customer>. We hear you. Being able to turn off web search is a our top priority for Ask GovernAI right now.

 

The team is moving fast. Recently we launched Ask GovernAI in <region>, <region 2> and <region 3>, added cross-region inference (CRIS), and brought Ask GovernAI to the iPad. We are working as quickly as we can on the two items <customer> raised.

 

1. Turn off web search


The admin controls to turn off web search are targeted for <date>. We are working to bring that date forward, and <PM> will update you as soon as it moves.A package change is not the way to do this. It would remove other Ask GovernAI features <customer> will want. The admin control is the right fix.
 

2. Resource Centre search


Team is close to doing final testing, and we are targeting the same time range. We will confirm as soon as it is live on the <customer> site.
`````

## Why it works

- He opens with a short acknowledgement ("We hear you") and states the top priority. There is no apology paragraph.
- He lists recent launches (<region>, <region 2>, <region 3>, CRIS, iPad) to show speed before he gives the date.
- He gives the real date and no more. The product lead, named in full, owns any update.
- He closes off the "package change" idea with a reason: it removes features the customer wants.
- The Resource Centre line is hedged ("targeting the same time range"). It avoids a date he cannot keep.
- There is no sign-off. This is Teams chat style.
