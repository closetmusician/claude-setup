## Situation

The <region> sales VP wants to know what <customer> can test and where it is hosted.
He also wants a side-by-side view of the old and new products.
His team waits on product before it builds training and collateral.
Yu-Kuan answers the night before his PTO.

## Why it works

- He frames the answer as "our overall strategy" in three numbered steps. He does not answer each question one by one.
- He leads with good news ("<region> recently got the latest LLM models").
- He hands over finished materials with one line per file. He marks the internal prep doc as not for sharing.
- The "Actions" list gives <sales VP> and <PMM> one verb each. Ownership moves to the field team.
- He names backup contacts for his PTO and says "really looking to your team's leadership from this point on". That line is the hand-off.
- He gives hosting in one line each (<city> for the sandbox, <region 2> today for Ask GovernAI). He leaves the security process to the security lead's documents.
