2. Strategic (−78%) and Mid-Market (−71%): different failures

Strategic: a real AI-upgrade conversion stall, not a proven definitional artefact

"Strategic" is a CS account-coverage tag — it lands on exactly 46 in-scope July opportunities and is null on the other 573; all 46 are Upsell/Cross-Sell, zero New, so "STRAT has no Land" is true by construction.9 The whole segment produces about 10 wins a month globally across 43 accounts, so the headline swings on timing:

  • AMS STRAT "$2K, −99%" is two sub-$5K contract amendments (TriSummit $1,037 + BCBS Wyoming $892 = $1,929) — while AMS Strategic's one real deal, Disney Worldwide Services at $102,000, was still open at month-end. Had it closed, AMS STRAT would read ~$104K.9, 10
  • EMEA STRAT ($92K) is real but concentrated — five referral wins, of which Argenta Holdings ($51K, itself a D1P conversion) is 55%.9, 11
  • Every material AMS Strategic loss is a GovernAI upgrade that deferred — for not-yet-GA features or the ~$25–35K price (see the AMER section) — not a lost bake-off (only 2 of 26 losses touch a competitor, both under $5K).10

The deep root cause is a self-inflicted GTM/roadmap-timing mismatch: reps sell the AI vision (AI Board Member, Smart Search) as near-term and price the ~$25–35K interim GovernAI upgrade as pre-paying for that roadmap — but shipped GovernAI is admin summarisation the cosecs already do by hand, so there is no cost to waiting and the AI-hungry Strategic buyer rationally defers to the flagship (actual GA Q1 2027).10, 17 One honest limit, from the adversarial review: we cannot prove a definitional artefact caused the −78% YoY — there is no July-2025 baseline, no documented Strategic-assignment rule and no reclassification bridge, and buyers waiting for a differently-priced future product may be genuine effective-demand losses at today's offer, not preserved pipeline.9 Note the entanglement with Enterprise: folding Strategic into Enterprise moves 24 deals / $272K, so part of ENT's +197% is reclassification.9

Why SMB grew while MM collapsed under the same pressure

Four structural reasons: (1) the package ladder — Starter/Foundations are ~100% SMB; MM new logos get only Essential/Pro, so SMB has an on-ramp and MM has a cliff; (2) the D1P wave lifted SMB (17 deals) and Enterprise (14) but bypassed MM (4); (3) SMB's motion is majority new-logo referral that works, while MM's engine is Expand, which fell from 28.7% to 2.4% clean; (4) coverage — AMS MM teams went 0W/41L and EMEA has no MM territory at all.9 SMB has not "solved" price-to-value; it has a product ladder and a channel that fit its buyer. MM has neither.

Driver 1 — Price-to-Value (the #1 driver). APAC's −4.4pt miss is the mirror image of EMEA: no whale.
