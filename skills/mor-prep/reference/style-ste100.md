# Style — cautiously-optimistic tone + ASD-STE100 plain English

Applies to all prose an executive reads, and **strictly** to the deck exec summary (slide 58-class).

## Tone — cautiously optimistic, tempered
- **Lead with what genuinely went well** before the risks: target beats, strong segments, real
  growth. If the month beat target, the first sentence says so.
- State risks **factually, without drama**. Banned framings: "the sky is falling", "hollow",
  "shrinking/breaking funnel", "worst in the company", "best-looking number in the deck", "masks",
  "collapse" as a verb for anything that merely declined.
- The honest posture after adversarial review is usually "a healthy month with understood
  watch-items", not "a business in trouble". Only escalate the tone if something is genuinely wrong
  and proven.
- Do not editorialise about PMF, another team's performance, or strategy beyond what the evidence
  states. Write the artifact; let the numbers carry the judgement.

## Language — ASD-STE100 (Simplified Technical English)
Write for a smart 18-year-old. Keep every figure and caveat precise; make the sentence simple.
- **Short sentences** — descriptive ≤ 25 words; one idea per sentence.
- **Active voice.** "Existing customers upgraded", not "upgrades were made".
- **Simple present/past tense.** Avoid complex/continuous verb forms where a simple one works.
- **One word = one meaning; use common words.** No unexplained jargon; if a term is unavoidable,
  define it in one clause ("GDR (gross dollar retention)"). Use consistent terms — do not swap
  synonyms for the same thing.
- **Keep articles** (the/a). **Avoid noun clusters** of >3 nouns (product names excepted).
- **No contractions** in the deck exec summary ("do not", not "don't"). Full sentences, not telegraphic.
- Numerals for data; spell nothing that loses precision. Positive statements where possible.

### Example — the same fact, before and after
- ❌ (AI-slop / alarmist): "Bookings beat masks a shrinking funnel; the growth is hollow, riding a
  base-conversion wave that is collapsing."
- ✅ (STE100 / tempered): "July bookings were $1.73M. This is 4% above target and 17% above the year
  plan. Enterprise and SMB were both strong. Most growth came from one move: existing customers
  upgraded to the Acme One Platform to use the new AI tools. The growth is real. It slowed this
  month, and each customer upgrades only once — so we must keep new-customer sales healthy."

### Deck exec-summary bullets must LEAD WITH THE WHY
Each recap bullet opens with the root cause; the number supports it. Do not append the "why" as a
trailing parenthetical to a "what" sentence — that reads as an afterthought and was a rejected draft.
